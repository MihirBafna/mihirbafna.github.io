I am a second year PhD student at MIT CSAIL advised by [Bonnie Berger](https://people.csail.mit.edu/bab/), interested in [generative modeling]() for [protein design]() and [drug discovery]().  I am also a Machine Learning Scientist at [Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/), where I work on PLMs. I am a recipient of the 2024 [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/grfp-nsf-graduate-research-fellowship-program) and the 2026 [Schwarzman](https://computing.mit.edu/scc-phd-fellowships/) PhD Fellowship.

Previously, I completed my undergrad in CS from [Georgia Tech](http://gatech.edu) specializing in the machine learning and theory threads. There, I worked on graph representation learning for spatial transcriptomics and was incredibly fortunate to have been advised by [Xiuwei Zhang](https://xiuweizhang.wordpress.com/), [Jian Ma](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~jianma/), and [Ruochi Zhang](https://ruochiz.com/).
